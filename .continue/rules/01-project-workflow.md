# Project Workflow Guardrails

- Finish tasks end-to-end where feasible instead of stopping at partial analysis.
- Prefer precise, minimal edits that preserve existing structure and style.
- For content tasks, maintain consistency with existing site tone and formatting.
- When editing technical or content files, validate assumptions by reading nearby context first.
- If a requested change could break site behaviour, call out the risk and provide a safe alternative.
- Avoid destructive operations unless explicitly requested.
- For long tasks, provide concise progress updates and keep momentum until complete.

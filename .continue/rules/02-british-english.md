# British English Standards

- Use British English spellings exclusively.
- Prefer -ise and -isation forms (organise, realise, optimisation).
- Prefer -our endings (colour, behaviour, labour).
- Prefer -re endings where applicable (centre, metre, theatre).
- Use noun and verb distinctions correctly: licence (noun), license (verb); practice (noun), practise (verb).
- Before finalising content, check for common US variants and convert to UK forms.

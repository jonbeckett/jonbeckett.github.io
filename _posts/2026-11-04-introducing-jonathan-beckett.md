Hello, and welcome. I'm Jonathan Beckett: software and web developer by day, aviation enthusiast and virtual pilot whenever I can be. I'm **not a real pilot**, but I enjoy learning about aircraft and sharing what I discover in the world of flight simulation.

## From an 8-bit cockpit to the virtual skies

My interest in flight simulation goes back to the mid-1980s, when I tried *Flight Sim 737* on an 8-bit computer. A few years later, I was flying *Sublogic Flight Simulator II* on an Atari ST. I kept coming back to simulators on the PC over the years, although there was a long pause while my daughters were young and I didn't have a computer capable of running one.

The journey really took off again when I inherited a computer from my Dad. He and his friends in a virtual airline were learning to fly the ZiboMod 737 in X-Plane, so I made a few tutorial videos to help them. I had no idea that those first videos would lead to so many more.

## Sharing what I learn

In the years since, I've made more than a thousand videos and built a community of nearly 50,000 fellow virtual aviators on YouTube. I make tutorials, share aircraft and simulator discoveries, write guides and flight plans, and livestream flights with the community. The subjects range from learning a cockpit's systems to planning a route and seeing where a flight takes us.

I still work as a software and web developer for a Microsoft partner, building large systems whose details I can't usually share. Flight simulation is a very different kind of problem-solving: there's always another aircraft to learn, another procedure to understand, or another corner of the world to explore.

## What you'll find here

This website will become my homepage in the new year - a jumping off point for everything I do online. Whether you're new to flight simulation or have been flying virtual skies for years, I hope you'll find something useful, interesting, or at least entertaining via the links on the front page.

Thanks for stopping by. I hope to see you in the virtual skies!
